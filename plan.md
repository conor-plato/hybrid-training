## Block 1: Foundation (9 Jun - 30 Aug 2026, 12 weeks)

**Job:** Rebuild size and strength on familiar barbell movements. Lay aerobic base. Put muscle back on.

### Weekly structure

| Day | AM | PM |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Pull (garage)** - DL heavy + back compounds | — |
| Tue | Easy Z2 run 30-45 min | **Legs (commercial)** - hack squat, leg press, lunges, curls/extensions, calves |
| Wed | **Push (garage)** - Bench heavy + push compounds | — |
| Thu | Easy Z2 run 30-45 min | **Pull (commercial)** - rows, pulldowns, face pulls, biceps, rear delts |
| Fri | **Tempo run** - 5 wu + 15-25 min tempo + 5 cd | — |
| Sat | Recovery / mobility | **Push (commercial)** - incline DB, chest press, fly, tris, side delts |
| Sun | **Long Z2 run** 50-90 min continuous | — |

### Volume + intensity rules

**Compound lifts (AM, garage):**
- Mon DL: 5x6 @ 70% of 3RM in Wk 1, climbing to 4x4 @ 85% by wk 12
- Wed Bench: 5x6 @ ~85kg in Wk 1, climbing to 4x4 @ ~100kg by wk 12

**Accessories (PM, commercial):**
Programmed by muscle group, not by exercise. You pick what to do based on gym setup and feel — just hit the target sets per muscle.

- Tue Legs PM: Quads 6 sets, Hams 3 sets, Glutes 3 sets, Calves 4 sets, Abs 3 sets
- Thu Pull PM: Mid-back 4, Lats 4, Rear delts 3, Biceps 4, Forearms 2 (optional)
- Sat Push PM: Chest 6, Triceps 4, Side delts 3, Rear delts 2

Same numbers on the AM compound days for the post-compound accessory blocks. Rep range 8-15 across the board, RPE 7-9 on the last set of each muscle group.

**Running:**
- Easy + long: Z2 (talk-test). HR cap
- Tempo: Z4 (comfortably hard, short phrases). Builds from 15 min to 25 min over 12 weeks
- Long Z2: 50 min wk 1 → 90 min by wk 12

### Milestones in this block

| Date | Marker |
|---|---|
| Wk 4 (6 Jul) | DL working +20-30kg over baseline. Bench at 90-95kg. Long run 60 min |
| Wk 8 (3 Aug) | DL working +40-50kg. Bench at ~100kg. Long run 75 min |
| Wk 12 (30 Aug) | **DL 3RM ~240-250kg, Bench 3RM ~135-145kg, 5k TT ~22:00, long run 90 min** |

### Exit criteria
Test week at wk 12. New 3RMs on DL and bench, 5k TT, weight check. Numbers feed Block 2.

## Block 3: Hyrox Peak (2 Nov 2026 - race day, 12-14 weeks)

**Job:** Get to the start line sharp, sub-60 capable, with strength on maintenance.

### Weekly structure

Strength drops to 2 quality sessions per week (DL Mon + Bench Wed), keeping intensity but cutting volume. Hyrox is the headline. Run sessions stay quality.

| Day | AM | PM |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **DL maintenance** - top single/triple at 85-90% + backdown | — |
| Tue | 5k pace work | **Legs (commercial, lighter)** |
| Wed | **Bench maintenance** - top single/triple + backdown | — |
| Thu | **Hyrox specific** (kitted gym) - simulation, compromised running | **Pull (commercial)** |
| Fri | **Compromised running intervals** - 800m run + station + repeat | — |
| Sat | Recovery / mobility | **Push (commercial)** |
| Sun | **Long Z2 run with race flow** - mock Hyrox once a month | — |

### Specific Hyrox preparation

The race is 8 x 1km runs separated by 8 stations: ski erg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, rower, farmer carry, sandbag lunges, wall balls. Practice in order at full Hyrox loads (24kg sled men's regular, 9kg wall ball).

### Critical taper
Last 7 days before race: no heavy lifts, no hard intervals. Two short race-pace efforts mid-week. Sleep extra. Carb up 48h out.

### Milestones in this block

| Date | Marker |
|---|---|
| Wk 4 (30 Nov) | First Hyrox sim done. Time TBC |
| Wk 8 (28 Dec) | Sim under 62 min |
| Wk 12-14 (Jan/Feb 2027) | **Race day: sub-60** |

### Exit criteria
Race completed. Take a full week off any structured training afterward.

- Third attempt: 305kg if 300 felt clean, repeat 300 if grind
- Phone recording. Someone with you. Chalk bowl. Music
- Sleep 9 hours the night before. No alcohol for 5 days prior
