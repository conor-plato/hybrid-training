## Block 1: Foundation (9 Jun - 30 Aug 2026, 12 weeks)

**Job:** Rebuild strength back toward old PBs. Lay aerobic base. Put muscle back on.

### Weekly structure
4 strength sessions, 3 runs, 1 of those is low-fatigue. Bodybuilding accessories on every strength day, with Sunday biased toward upper-body pump work.

| Day | Session | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Deadlift day** | Heavy DL working sets, rows, lat work, biceps, abs. 75-90 min |
| Tue | **Easy Z2 run** | 30-45 min conversational. Mobility after |
| Wed | **Bench day** | Heavy bench working sets, triceps, shoulders, more biceps. 75-90 min |
| Thu | **Quality run** | Tempo or fartlek 35-45 min, or easy if Tue was hard |
| Fri | **Legs + Hyrox prep** | Moderate squat/leg press volume, walking lunges, sandbag carries, wall ball subs. 60-75 min |
| Sat | **Long Z2 run** | 45 min in wk 1 building to 75-90 min by wk 12 |
| Sun | **Upper pump day** | Arms, back, shoulders, low fatigue, RPE 6-7. 45-60 min. Swap for walk + mobility on a heavy fatigue week |

### Volume + intensity rules
- DL: Build from 3-4 working sets of 6 at 70% of baseline 3RM, progress to sets of 5 at 75-80% by wk 12
- Bench: Same shape, sets of 6-8 at 70-75%, progress to sets of 5 at 80%
- Running: Mostly Z2 (HR cap, conversational). One quality session per week
- Accessories: 3-4 sets each, 8-15 rep range, RPE 7-9

### Milestones in this block

| Date | Marker |
|---|---|
| Wk 4 (6 Jul) | Working sets 20-30kg above baseline. Z2 long run at 60 min comfortably |
| Wk 8 (3 Aug) | Working sets 40-50kg above baseline. Long run at 75 min |
| Wk 12 (30 Aug) | **DL 3RM 240-250kg, Bench 3RM 140-150kg, 5k TT around 22:00, long run 75-90 min** |

### Exit criteria
Test week at wk 12. New 3RMs on DL and bench, 5k TT, weight check. Numbers fed into Block 2.

## Block 2: Build (31 Aug - 22 Nov 2026, 12 weeks)

**Job:** Pass your old PBs. Ramp Hyrox prep. Run a focused 5k speed block.

### Weekly structure
Strength stays 3x with one heavy session each on DL/bench/legs. Hyrox-specific work goes from 0 to 1-2 sessions per week as you get gym access. Running adds a quality block.

| Day | Session | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **DL day** | Heavy DL, fewer working sets, higher % |
| Tue | **5k speed session** | Intervals, threshold, or repeats. The 5k goes from base-built to fast here |
| Wed | **Bench day** | Heavy bench + upper accessories |
| Thu | **Hyrox specific** | Sled push/pull, ski erg, rower, wall balls, lunges. From Oct onwards once kit access is sorted |
| Fri | **Squat + Hyrox accessory** | Squat moderate volume + Hyrox movement work (sandbag, wall balls) |
| Sat | **Long run with race elements** | 60-90 min Z2, optional pickups, mock Hyrox stations once a month |
| Sun | **Pump day or rest** | Read your HRV/RHR. If trending poor, take it as rest |

### Volume + intensity rules
- DL: Sets of 3-5 in the 80-90% range. Old PB +/- 5% by mid-block
- Bench: Same. Old PB tied or passed by week 8
- 5k: Specific speed work. One key session weekly. Threshold runs, 400m repeats, mile repeats
- Hyrox: Build from 30 min sessions to 50-60 min sessions. Practice transitions

### Critical setup before this block
- **By 28 Sep 2026:** Find and book a Hyrox-equipped gym in Chch (Les Mills, CityFitness Riccarton, or similar). At least 1 session/week from October on. Without this, sub-60 is fitness-only and shaky.

### Milestones in this block

| Date | Marker |
|---|---|
| Wk 4 (28 Sep) | DL working at 230-250kg, first proper Hyrox session done |
| Wk 8 (26 Oct) | DL single at 260-270kg, Bench at 155-165kg, Hyrox sim once |
| Wk 12 (22 Nov) | **DL 275kg+ (old PB tied), Bench 165kg+ (old PB tied), 5k 20:30-21:00, Hyrox time-check sub-65** |

### Exit criteria
Strength PRs verified. Hyrox sim run end of block at a kitted gym to set a baseline race time.

## Block 3: Hyrox Peak (23 Nov 2026 - race day, 8-10 weeks)

**Job:** Get to the start line sharp, sub-60 capable, with strength on maintenance not in decline.

### Weekly structure
Strength drops to 2 quality sessions per week (DL day + Bench day), keeping intensity but cutting volume. Hyrox is the headline. Run sessions stay quality.

| Day | Session | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **DL maintenance** | Top single or top triple at 85-90%, then 1-2 backdown sets. Short, sharp |
| Tue | **5k pace work** | Threshold or VO2 work depending on the week |
| Wed | **Bench maintenance** | Same shape as DL: top single/triple + backdown |
| Thu | **Hyrox specific** | Full simulation work at the kitted gym. Compromised running between stations |
| Fri | **Compromised running** | Run-station intervals. Run 800m, 25 wall balls, run 800m, 50 lunges, etc |
| Sat | **Long run with race flow** | Once a month run a full mock Hyrox |
| Sun | **Race-day prep / mobility** | Light. Sleep priority |

### Specific Hyrox preparation
The race is 8 x 1km runs separated by 8 stations: ski erg, sled push, sled pull, burpee broad jumps, rower, farmer carry, sandbag lunges, wall balls. Practice these in order, multiple times, at full Hyrox loads (24kg sled men's regular, 9kg wall ball, etc.).

### Critical taper
Last 7 days before race: no heavy lifts, no hard intervals. Two short race-pace efforts mid-week. Sleep extra. Carb up 48h out.

### Milestones in this block

| Date | Marker |
|---|---|
| Wk 4 (21 Dec) | First full Hyrox sim under 65 min |
| Wk 6 (4 Jan) | Sim under 62 min |
| Wk 8-10 (Jan/Feb) | **Race day: sub-60** |

### Exit criteria
Race completed. Take a full week off any structured training afterward. Walk, sleep, eat. Recovery is the work for that week.

## Block 4: Hypertrophy Reset (post-race - 28 Mar 2027, ~6 weeks)

**Job:** Rebuild after the race. Put muscle back on. Reset for the strength push.

### Weekly structure
Volume back, intensity down. Bodybuilding flavour throughout. Runs at maintenance only.

| Day | Session | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **DL volume** | Sets of 6-8 at 70-75%. RPE 7-8. 4-5 sets total |
| Tue | **Easy Z2 run** | 30-40 min. Just keeping the engine ticking |
| Wed | **Bench volume** | Sets of 8 at 70-75%. Heavier triceps focus |
| Thu | **Light run or rest** | Optional easy 30 min or true rest |
| Fri | **Squat/leg volume + BB** | Squat sets of 6-8, then quad/glute/hamstring work |
| Sat | **Long easy run or arm/back pump** | Pick based on fatigue. 60 min max |
| Sun | **Pump day** | Full upper-body BB. Higher rep, low load, lots of isolation. 60 min |

### Volume + intensity rules
- DL/Bench/Squat: 4-5 working sets of 6-8 reps at 70-75% of est. 1RM. The point is muscle, not numbers
- Lots of isolation accessory work: rows, lat pulldowns, curls, tris, lateral raises, leg curls, leg ext
- Runs: 3 easy sessions max. No speed work. Just keep aerobic base

### Milestones in this block

| Date | Marker |
|---|---|
| Wk 3 | Full volume tolerated, no race hangover |
| Wk 6 (28 Mar) | **DL 3RM at 260kg, Bench 3RM at 155kg, weight back up 2-3kg if it dropped during Hyrox prep** |

### Exit criteria
Three test sets at end of block to gauge readiness for intensification.

## Block 5: Intensification (29 Mar - 30 May 2027, 9 weeks)

**Job:** Move into heavier loads, lower reps. Test the strength ceiling.

### Weekly structure
4 lift / 2 run / 1 rest. Running drops to maintenance. Strength is the priority.

| Day | Session | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **DL heavy** | Sets of 3-5 at 80-90% |
| Tue | **Easy run 30 min** | Maintenance only |
| Wed | **Bench heavy** | Sets of 3-5 at 80-90% |
| Thu | **Light upper + arms** | Recovery-style upper-body BB, 45 min |
| Fri | **Squat + DL accessory** | Squat sets of 3-5, plus DL-specific accessories (paused DLs, deficit DLs, halt pulls) |
| Sat | **Easy run or rest** | 30 min Z2 or full rest |
| Sun | **Rest or mobility** | True rest day if hammered, otherwise mobility |

### Volume + intensity rules
- DL: Sets of 3-5 at 80-90%. Weekly progression of 2.5-5kg on top sets
- Bench: Same shape
- One AMRAP set every 3rd week to test pace of progress
- Accessories trimmed back to what supports DL/bench

### Milestones in this block

| Date | Marker |
|---|---|
| Wk 4 (27 Apr) | DL working at 260-275kg, Bench 155-165kg |
| Wk 8 (25 May) | DL single 280kg, Bench single 170kg |
| Wk 9 (30 May) | **DL test: 285kg single. Bench test: 170kg single** |

### Exit criteria
Confident singles in those ranges. Numbers feed the peaking cycle weights.

## Block 6: Peaking + 300kg attempt (31 May - 18 Aug 2027, 11 weeks)

**Job:** Sharpen the deadlift to a 300kg single. Bench peak too. Everything else is supporting cast.

### Weekly structure
DL and bench are the spine. Volume drops, intensity climbs week on week. Last week is deload.

| Day | Session | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **DL singles/doubles** | The headline lift. Top single at 90-95%, sometimes a deload set |
| Tue | **Walk or full rest** | Recovery |
| Wed | **Bench singles/doubles** | Same shape as DL |
| Thu | **Light DL technique** | Halt pulls, paused pulls, or speed pulls at 60-70%. Movement quality only |
| Fri | **Light bench or accessory** | Touch-and-go bench at 70%, or just BB upper accessory |
| Sat | **Walk** | Active recovery. No real session |
| Sun | **Rest** | Genuinely rest |

### Peaking progression (DL only, bench follows the same shape)

| Week | DL top set | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wk 1 (31 May) | 275-280kg single | Re-acclimate to heavy |
| Wk 2 | 280kg double | Volume at near-max |
| Wk 3 | 285kg single | Test the recent intensification number |
| Wk 4 | 270kg x 3 | Light week, technique |
| Wk 5 (29 Jun) | 290kg single | Stretching the ceiling |
| Wk 6 | 285kg double | Volume |
| Wk 7 | 292-295kg single | Brushing 300 |
| Wk 8 | 275kg x 3 | Light week |
| Wk 9 (27 Jul) | 295kg single | Confidence single |
| Wk 10 | 280kg x 2 | Volume down, intensity up |
| Wk 11 (10 Aug) | **DELOAD** | Top set 200kg max, lots of stretching, sleep, eat |
| **Tue 18 Aug 2027** | **300kg ATTEMPT** | Birthday. The day |

### Attempt day protocol
- Eat clean carbs 2-3h before. Caffeine 30 min out
- Warmup ramp: bar, 60, 100, 140, 180, 220, 260, 280
- First attempt: 285kg (confidence builder)
- Second attempt: 300kg
- Third attempt: 305kg if 300 felt clean, repeat 300 if 300 was a grind
- Have a phone recording. Have someone with you. Have a chalk bowl. Have your music
- Sleep 9 hours the night before. No alcohol for 5 days prior

### Exit criteria
300kg lifted. (And if not, 295kg + a video to refine for next attempt 6 weeks later.)

## Post-300kg: what comes next

This is open. Some options:

- **Marathon prep starts Sept 2027.** 16-20 week build to a marathon in Jan/Feb 2028. Strength drops to maintenance, running takes over.
- **Ultra prep.** Longer ramp, probably 20-24 weeks for a 50k. Mid-2028 race.
- **305kg push.** If the 300 felt straightforward, a focused 8-week cycle could put 305-310kg on the table.
- **Hyrox revisit.** Now with full strength back and a year of conditioning behind you, sub-55 is realistic.

We pick when we get there. Closer to the date your priorities will be clearer.
